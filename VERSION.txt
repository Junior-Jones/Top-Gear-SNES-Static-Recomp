Version 2.1.1

General,
* Menu fix: The original menu layout no longer flashes when using Up, Down, Left or Right.
* Time Trial menu fixes: The L/R shoulder buttons now change the car filter correctly. Results text stays inside the menu and no longer leaves stray digits on the leaderboard.
* Timing data fix: A corrupt or unreadable timing file is protected from being overwritten.
* Settings improvements: Options forced by 2 player mode stay silent and do not change your saved 1 player settings.
* Menu spacing: Fixed leftover difficulty text and moved the Center Screen message inside the border.
* Menu sounds: Pages with only one choice stay silent when Up or Down cannot change the selection.

Feedback on any issues is welcomed
