Top Gear Definitive Edition 2.1.0 - final display-option rules

Fresh defaults: Bottom Screen On, Center Screen Off, Extra CPUs On, FPS Off.
Center Screen can be enabled only after Bottom Screen is Off. Turning Bottom
Screen On disables Center Screen. Disabled Center Screen controls are silent.
Its solo lock still prevents two-player Career/Rally while enabled.

One-player lower view with Bottom Screen On
------------------------------------------
Career: TOP 5 CHECKPOINT GAPS. Five driver names in race order and their time
gaps to the leader at measured checkpoints. The leader row shows the last lap
time when available; an unavailable value is a placeholder.
Rally: the same TOP 5 CHECKPOINT GAPS race display, for the current Rally race.
Trial: current lap number, current lap elapsed time, S1/S2/S3 sector times,
and eight completed-lap time rows. Uncompleted sectors/laps show placeholders.
There is no opponent standings panel in Trial.

Bottom Screen Off hides the lower raster and stats. Center Screen On then
moves P1 into the middle at original size, with both 60-line margins black.
No live stats are drawn below the middle view. P1's own HUD remains visible.
Two-player Career/Rally retain the effective Bottom Screen On override so P2
can see their own viewport.
